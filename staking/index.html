How it works
• The full reward amount is sent to the pool when you create it and can never be taken back.
• Rewards are paid out every second, split by stake size. The period starts with the first staker and pauses while nobody is staked.
• With a lock, each stake (and each new top-up) is locked for that many days. Rewards can still be claimed any time. Stakers can always leave early with an emergency withdraw, giving up their pending rewards to the remaining stakers.
• Tokens with transfer tax: the pool counts what actually arrives. Rebasing/reflection tokens are not supported.
Optional. Unlisted pools work exactly the same; you share their link yourself. You can list or remove it later. The listing fee is paid once per pool.

How the tax works
• Maximum 5% for buys and 5% for sells, steps of 0.01%. You can lower it later, but never raise it.
• Tax is charged only on buys and sells in the token's PancakeSwap V2 BNB pair, which is created automatically with your token. Wallet-to-wallet transfers are tax-free.
• The tax is paid in your token and sent directly to the tax wallet (it is not swapped to BNB).
• Adding the first liquidity is tax-free. Add it on PancakeSwap V2.
